# Guidr pilot: consent and data terms for applicants

> **Status:** draft v0 for owner review (issue #52), 2026-11-07. This is not legal advice. See
> `data-handling.md` §9 for where a lawyer should review before the first applicant signs.
>
> **How to use:** send this text to the applicant before anything else. Record their answer
> (signature, or a dated "I agree" reply by email) in the private pilot folder. **No portfolio
> sheet is created and no data is collected until consent is recorded.**

---

## What the pilot is

During the pilot, Guidr's advisor helps you run your applications to funded research degrees
(PhD and research master's). For each program you apply to, the advisor keeps a list of your
deadlines, documents and next steps, and checks the program's key facts (deadline, funding,
eligibility for your citizenship, tests, documents, application fee, supervisor contact) against
the program's own website or staff, as described in Guidr's verification policy. Each fact is
marked as verified, unverified or "could not confirm".

**What the pilot does not do:**

- It **gives no guarantee of admission or funding.**
- It gives **no estimate of your chances** of admission or of receiving funding.
- It does not write or rewrite your essays or statements.
- It does not submit applications for you.

## What we collect and why

We collect only what is needed to run your applications.

**About you (from the qualification screen):**

| Information | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Your name and email address | To contact you and hold weekly reviews |
| Country of citizenship | Funding and eligibility rules often depend on it |
| Countries you are applying to | To know which rules and deadlines apply |
| Degree type and field of study | To confirm the pilot fits your plans |
| Whether your degree is completed or in its final year | Pilot eligibility |
| When your first deadline falls | To schedule your place in the pilot |

**About each application (your portfolio sheet):** program name, institution, destination
country, degree type, application stage (considering, preparing, submitted, interview, decision,
withdrawn), each milestone and its kind (deadline, document, test, recommendation, outreach,
other), who owns it, its due date, what it depends on, its status and completion date, and any
notes you or the advisor add.

**About each program's facts (program cards):** these describe the program, not you.

**We do not ask for:** grades, test scores, passport or identity documents, financial
information, health information, or your essays, unless you choose to share something for a
specific task. Do not send documents we have not asked for.

## Where it is stored and who can see it

- Your portfolio sheet and these records are kept in a private, access-controlled
  `<OWNER TO CONFIRM: storage location, e.g. "Google Drive folder owned by Guidr">`. They are
  never stored in Guidr's code repository.
- **Who can see them:** the advisor (Guidr's founder) and the second checker
  `<OWNER TO NAME: role or opaque reference>`, who re-checks key program facts. Nobody else.
- Program facts (deadlines, funding rules) may be shared between applicants applying to the same
  program. Your personal information is never shared with other applicants.
- Guidr does not sell your information or share it with universities, partners or advertisers.

## Use of AI services

During the pilot, **your personal information is not sent to any AI or model provider** unless you
give separate written permission for a specific task. Research on program facts is done about the
program, not about you. See `ai-data-use.md` for the detail.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information **until 90 days after the pilot ends**, then delete it, except for any
outcome information you agree below to let us keep.

## Your rights: copy, correction and deletion

You can ask at any time for a copy of your information, for a correction, or for deletion, by
emailing **hello@guidr.app**. We will reply within **14 days**. If you ask for deletion before the
pilot ends, we stop working on your applications.

## Refund

If the pilot cohort does not run, your payment is refunded in full.
